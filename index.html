Meet Dzifa

Beauty with a designer’s eye.

I’m Francisca Dzifa Afanyede, a fashion designer, makeup artist and hairstylist with eight years of experience bringing complete looks together.

With a Bachelor of Arts in Fashion and Textile Design, I approach beauty with an understanding of colour, texture and balance. I look at how your hair and makeup complement your outfit, your features and the way you want to feel.

At Zef Beauty, your ideas are the starting point. Whether you’re drawn to soft, understated makeup, a bold statement or a carefully coordinated bridal look, we’ll shape the details around your personal style and occasion.

My aim is simple: for you to look in the mirror, recognise yourself and love what you see.
